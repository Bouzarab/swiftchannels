Installation guide

Watching in about
ten minutes.

Find your device below and follow the steps. Every device works the same way: install a player app, then enter the details we sent you. If anything looks different on your screen, message us and we will walk you through it.

01 Your details from us

After you order we send you what your device needs on WhatsApp — either an activation code, or a login made of a server address, a username and a password. Which one depends on the device. Use whatever is in our message.

02 A steady connection

About 15 Mbps is enough for HD and 25 Mbps for 4K. A cable to the TV or router is steadier than Wi-Fi, and a 5 GHz Wi-Fi band is steadier than 2.4 GHz.

03 Ten minutes

Most people are watching within ten minutes. Do it once and the app remembers everything — you will not have to enter anything again.

Samsung & LG smart TV

IBO Player · Smart IPTV · IPTV Smarters
  1. Open the app store on your TV — Samsung Apps on a Samsung, LG Content Store on an LG.
  2. Search for IBO Player and install it. If you cannot find it, try Smart IPTV, Set IPTV or IPTV Smarters — any of them works, and we support all four.
  3. Open the app. It will show you a device key or a MAC address on the screen. Send that to us on WhatsApp.
  4. We link your subscription to that key from our side. It takes a minute or two.
  5. Close the app fully and open it again. Your channels will be there.

Note: these TV apps usually charge a small one-off activation fee of a few euros, paid to the app maker, not to us. It is charged once per TV and it is not part of your subscription.

Amazon Firestick & Fire TV

Downloader → TiviMate or IPTV Smarters
  1. From the Fire TV home screen, search for Downloader and install it.
  2. Go to Settings → My Fire TV → Developer options and turn on Install unknown apps for Downloader. On some remotes you first need to open Settings → My Fire TV → About and click the Fire TV name seven times to make Developer options appear.
  3. Open Downloader and enter the download address we send you. Install the player when it finishes.
  4. Open the player and enter the details from our message — the activation code, or the server address with your username and password.
  5. Give it a moment to load the channel list, then start watching.

Tip: TiviMate has the nicest programme guide on a Firestick; IPTV Smarters is simpler to set up. Tell us which you prefer and we will send the right link.

Android TV, Google TV & Android box

Google Play → TiviMate or IPTV Smarters
  1. Open the Google Play Store on the device.
  2. Search for TiviMate or IPTV Smarters Pro and install it.
  3. Open the app and choose to add a playlist or a new user.
  4. Enter the details from our message — the activation code, or the server address with your username and password.
  5. Wait for the channel list to load. That is it.

Note: on a cheaper Android box, turn the app's picture buffer up a little if the picture stutters. We can talk you through it on WhatsApp.

Android phone & tablet

Google Play → IPTV Smarters Pro
  1. Open the Google Play Store.
  2. Search for IPTV Smarters Pro and install it.
  3. Open the app and accept the terms it shows you.
  4. Choose to log in, then enter the details from our message — the activation code, or the server address with your username and password.
  5. Your channels, films and series appear in the app.

iPhone & iPad

App Store → IPTV Smarters Player Lite
  1. Open the App Store.
  2. Search for IPTV Smarters Player Lite and install it.
  3. Open the app and accept the terms it shows you.
  4. Enter the details from our message — the activation code, or the server address with your username and password.
  5. Your channels load and you are ready.

Note: Apple sometimes renames or removes these players. If you cannot find that exact name, message us and we will tell you which one is currently in the store.

Apple TV

App Store → IPTV Smarters Player Lite
  1. Open the App Store on your Apple TV. This needs a 4th-generation Apple TV or newer.
  2. Search for IPTV Smarters Player Lite and install it.
  3. Open the app and enter the details from our message.
  4. Typing with the Apple TV remote is slow — you can use the keyboard on your iPhone instead when it offers.
  5. Wait for the channel list, then start watching.

Windows & Mac

VLC or IPTV Smarters for PC
  1. Install VLC Media Player from videolan.org, or IPTV Smarters for PC if you want a proper TV interface with a programme guide.
  2. In VLC, open Media → Open Network Stream. In IPTV Smarters, choose to add a new user.
  3. Paste the playlist link we sent you, or enter the server address with your username and password.
  4. The channel list opens. In VLC you can save it as a favourite so it is one click next time.

Note: VLC plays the channels but has no programme guide and no catch-up. For the full experience on a computer, use IPTV Smarters.

If something is not right

The picture keeps stopping

Almost always the connection, not the service. Try a cable instead of Wi-Fi, or move closer to the router. If it only happens on one channel, tell us which one — we will check it from our side.

It says the login is wrong

Check for a capital letter or a space that crept in while typing. Copy and paste from our WhatsApp message rather than typing it out. If it still refuses, message us — we will resend it.

A channel I want is missing

Line-ups are set by the platform and do change. Send us the channel name and we will tell you whether it is carried and where to find it in the list.

Nothing loads at all

Some internet providers slow down streaming at busy times. Restart the router, then try again. If it is still dead, message us with your device and what you see on screen.
